📐 4 Linguistic Parameters of ISL Sign:

🖐️ Handshape B-Handshape (Flat open palms, fingers aligned)
📍 Signing Location Center of Chest / Sternum (Midline)
🔄 Movement Dynamic Symmetrical bilateral lift towards midline, gentle hold
😊 Facial Expression Warm smile with 15-degree polite forward head bow

📋 Step-by-Step Gesture Execution:

  1. Raise both hands to mid-chest height with open flat palms facing one another.
  2. Bring palms and fingers together smoothly at the sternum in a Namaste posture.
  3. Gently tilt head forward with a warm, respectful facial expression.
🏛️
Deaf Community Cultural Context:

In Indian Sign Language, Namaste is both an informal greeting and a universal mark of respect when welcoming guests, elders, and community members.

How Reverse Speech-to-Sign Works

Empowering hearing individuals to communicate with deaf community members
1
🎙️

1. Multilingual Voice Input

The hearing user speaks in English, Hindi, or Kannada. The neural speech recognition engine transcribes the acoustic signal into normalized text in real time.

2
🧠

2. Semantic Sign Parser

The NLP parser identifies single or compound phrases (such as "Hello how are you"), decomposes them into sign sequence tokens, and fetches the corresponding 3D motion keyframe dataset.

3
🤖

3. Neat 3D Avatar Signing

The 3D avatar executes smooth, articulated joint rotations with complete 5-finger tracking, high visual clarity, and clean unobstructed presentation.
