About Indian Sign Language

Indian Sign Language (ISL) is a complete, grammatically independent visual language relied upon by over 18 million Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals across the subcontinent.

Connecting Deaf Communities Across India

A Complete, Rich Language

ISL is not a direct translation of spoken languages; it possesses distinct spatial grammar, two-handed signing conventions, and expressive facial morphology.

Everyday Inclusion

Communication barriers often isolate individuals in classrooms, workplaces, and public spaces. ISL recognition bridges this divide with instantaneous translation.

Universal Interpreter

Human interpreters are scarce and costly. Our AI engine provides an anytime, anywhere digital interpreter right through any standard web camera.

Computer Vision & ML

Using 21 metric 3D landmarks per hand, our geometry-based feature engine ensures robust detection regardless of lighting, background clutter, or skin tone.

Processing Flow

How It Works

Three automated, high-speed stages transform physical hand signs into meaningful spoken sentences in real time.

01

Capture Gesture

Position your hands within the HD widescreen camera viewport. The system automatically detects both hands and maps 21 3D landmarks in real time.

02

AI Spatial Prediction

The 238-dimensional geometric vector (bend angles, spans, palm normal vector) is evaluated by XGBoost with sliding-window temporal smoothing.

03

Display & Speak

Signs construct complete sentences with auto-correction, predictive dictionary words, and instant vocalization in English, Hindi, or Kannada.

System Design & Pipeline

System Architecture

An end-to-end multi-tier pipeline connecting high-precision 3D computer vision, geometric feature engineering, gradient-boosted classification, and trilingual neural speech synthesis.

Integrated AI Architecture

Real-Time Spatial Hand Inference

Webcam video streams are tracked frame-by-frame by Google MediaPipe Tasks HandLandmarker. Extracted 3D points are normalized into wrist-centered 238-dimensional geometric features and classified by a low-latency gradient-boosted XGBoost model.

MediaPipe Tasks XGBoost Classifier Spatial Normalization Trilingual TTS
Pillar 01

Computer Vision Layer

Google MediaPipe Tasks HandLandmarker processes 60 FPS live video, mapping 21 spatial 3D keypoints per hand with sub-millimeter precision across single and two-handed ISL gestures.

MediaPipe • 21 3D Landmarks
Pillar 02

Geometric Feature Engine

Normalizes coordinates relative to the wrist and derives 238 invariant features: joint bend angles, inter-fingertip Euclidean distances, palm surface normals, and inter-hand spatial offsets.

238-D Feature Vector
Pillar 03

XGBoost Inference Engine

Optimized gradient-boosted decision trees deliver predictions in <35ms. Temporal probability averaging over a sliding window suppresses noise and guarantees reliable sign commitment.

Sub-35ms Latency • XGBoost
Pillar 04

NLP & Multilingual Speech

Sentence builder engine reconstructs grammar and auto-completes vocabulary from a 2,500+ word dictionary, streaming natural neural voices in English, Hindi, and Kannada.

Trilingual Neural TTS
