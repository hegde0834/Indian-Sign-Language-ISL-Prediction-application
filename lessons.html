Hello / Namaste

Greeting
👤 Human Signer: NCERT Official ISL Demonstrator
English: Hello / Welcome Hindi: नमस्ते / नमस्कार Kannada: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ / ಸುಸ್ವಾಗತ

The quintessential Indian Sign Language greeting and welcome. The human instructor joins both open flat palms smoothly at chest level in a respectful prayer/Namaste posture with a gentle forward head tilt and warm welcoming smile.

ISL Spatial Anatomy Components
🖐️ Handshape Flat Open Palms (B-handshape)
📍 Signing Location Center of Chest / Sternum
🔄 Movement Dynamic Hands meet at midline with slight extension
😊 Facial Expression Warm smile, polite forward head bow

Step-by-Step Signing Technique:

  1. Raise both hands to mid-chest height with open flat palms facing one another.
  2. Bring palms and fingers together smoothly at the sternum in a Namaste posture.
  3. Gently tilt head forward with a warm, respectful facial expression.
💡
In ISL, Namaste is both an informal 'hello' and a formal mark of respect when meeting elders, guests, and community members.
